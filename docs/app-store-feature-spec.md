# Sober Dailies — Feature Spec for App Store Copy

Verified against the 3.0.x codebase (branch `3.0.5-redesign`, July 2026). Everything listed here is live in the app; nothing planned or unreleased is included.

---

## Positioning (one paragraph)

Sober Dailies is a daily companion for people in A.A. recovery. It turns the suggestions of the program — prayer, meditation, literature, inventory, gratitude, connection with other alcoholics — into a simple daily practice you check off one day at a time. Everything lives in one place: the Big Book and Twelve & Twelve, Daily Reflections, speaker tapes, an AI sponsor available at 3 a.m., and a private journal of your whole journey. All personal data stays on your device (local-first, with optional iCloud / Google Drive backup).

---

## Feature areas

### 1. Today — your daily program
- **Sobriety counter**: a coin showing days sober, with years/months/days breakdown and your sober date. "One day at a time."
- **Daily Reflections**: today's reading front and center on a rotating photo hero; mark it Done when you've read it.
- **Your Dailies**: a customizable checklist of recovery practices grouped Morning / Anytime / Evening. Choose from a catalog (call my sponsor, morning/evening prayers, gratitude list, journal, read the literature, meditate, attend a meeting, nightly review, speaker tape, spot check, be of service, exercise, make my bed, talk with another alcoholic…) or create your own. Reorder, re-bucket, and edit anytime.
- **Call my sponsor**: save your sponsor from Contacts; call or text with one tap.
- **Sobriety birthday celebrations**: milestone modal on your A.A. birthdays.

### 2. AI Sponsor
- Three sponsor personas to choose from: **Steady Eddie** (patient, steady, wise), **Salty Sam** (old-school, direct, unvarnished), **Gentle Grace** (compassionate, gentle, encouraging).
- Streaming chat grounded in A.A. principles; available any hour. Quick-start chips: "I'm struggling today," "I'm fighting a craving," "I just need to vent," "Help me think this through."
- A persistent floating button gets you to your sponsor from anywhere in the app.
- Crisis safeguards: mentions of self-harm surface the 988 lifeline.

### 3. Spot Check Inventory (10th Step, on the spot)
- Guided 4-step inventory voiced by your chosen sponsor: name your feelings → describe where the day turned → look at your side of the street → get a summary with suggestions.
- Hands off directly into a sponsor chat to keep talking it through.

### 4. Nightly Review
- The Tenth Step nightly-review questions from *Alcoholics Anonymous* p. 86 ("When we retire at night…"), each as a write-in card.

### 5. Literature
- **Alcoholics Anonymous (Big Book, 4th ed.)** — full text and story sections. Full-text search with page numbers, go-to-page (1–575), bookmarks, and press-and-hold to **highlight** passages, add **notes**, copy, or share. Adjustable reading text size.
- **Twelve Steps and Twelve Traditions** — full reader with search, go-to-page, and bookmarks.
- **Daily Reflections** — today's reading plus a calendar to read any date; share a reflection.
- **Meeting Readings** — everything needed to chair a meeting: Meeting Script, How It Works, There Is a Solution, The Ninth Step Promises, A Vision for You, The Twelve Traditions.
- One shared **text size** control across all readers.

### 6. Speaker Tapes
- A library of A.A. speaker recordings: search by speaker/topic/hometown, weekly **featured speaker**, recently added, saved list.
- Player with playback speed, skip, save, and **download for offline** listening. Audio keeps playing while you use the rest of the app.

### 7. Meditation
- Full-screen meditation timer: 5/10/15/20 minutes or custom (1–90).
- Selectable **scenes** — ambient visuals with matching soundscapes (sunrise, snowfall, summer rain, and more streamed in).
- Breathing ring, closing bell, meditation minutes logged to your Journey.

### 8. Prayers
- Seven A.A. prayers (Morning, Third Step, Seventh Step, St. Francis, Serenity long form, Sick Man, Evening) plus **My Prayers** — add your own.

### 9. Journal & Gratitude
- Freeform **Journal** ("What's on your mind today?").
- **Gratitude list** — a numbered daily list.

### 10. Journey (your history)
- A day-by-day timeline of your recovery: Day N of sobriety, dailies completed, and every entry (gratitude, nightly review, spot check, journal) plus meditation minutes.
- Tap any day to read, edit recent days, or share an entry.
- **Trends**: active streaks per daily, a monthly progress heatmap, and insights (strongest day of the week, longest streak ever, average completion rate…).

### 11. Connection tools
- **Reach Out** — a personal call list of other alcoholics; add from Contacts, tap to call or text.
- **Meetings** — save your home group and regular meetings with a "Next up" card (join online / get directions), the official Meeting Guide finder, and an in-app browser of online meetings (aa-intergroup.org).

### 12. Pass It On
- Gift 3 months of Sober Dailies to a sponsee or newcomer, sent by text or share sheet; recipients redeem with a simple code. Plus a plain "share the app" link.

### 13. Privacy & data
- **Local-first**: journals, inventories, chat history, and your sober date live on your device — no account, no sign-up.
- Optional whole-app **backup & restore**: iCloud on iOS, Google Drive on Android.
- Light / Dark / System appearance; dark mode is a true OLED theme.

### 14. Subscription
- 7-day free trial, then monthly or yearly (yearly shows savings). Gift-code redemption ("Have a code?") and Restore Purchases.

## Draft full description

**Sober Dailies is a daily companion for your recovery.**

The program works when you work it — a little every day. Sober Dailies turns the suggestions you hear in the rooms into a simple daily practice: pray, meditate, read, take inventory, be grateful, and connect with another alcoholic. Check off your dailies one day at a time, and watch your streak grow.

**YOUR DAILIES**
Build a morning and evening routine from proven recovery practices — or add your own. A sobriety counter keeps your day count front and center, and milestone birthdays get celebrated.

**AN AI SPONSOR, ANY HOUR**
Choose the voice that fits you — patient Steady Eddie, no-nonsense Salty Sam, or encouraging Gentle Grace. Talk through a craving at 3 a.m., vent after a hard day, or take a guided Spot Check Inventory when something's got you off balance. (Your AI sponsor supplements — never replaces — a real sponsor and meetings.)

**THE LITERATURE, IN YOUR POCKET**
The Big Book and the Twelve & Twelve with full-text search, bookmarks, highlights, and notes. Today's Daily Reflection every morning. Every reading you need to chair a meeting.

**SPEAKER TAPES**
A growing library of A.A. speakers sharing experience, strength, and hope. Stream, save, or download talks for offline listening.

**QUIET TIME**
A beautiful meditation timer with ambient scenes and soundscapes. All the A.A. prayers, plus space for your own.

**PEN AND PAPER, MODERNIZED**
Journal, gratitude lists, the nightly review from page 86, and spot-check inventories — all collected in your Journey: a private day-by-day record of your recovery, with streaks, trends, and insights.

**STAY CONNECTED**
Keep a call list of program friends, save your home group and regular meetings, find meetings with Meeting Guide, and join online meetings right in the app.

**PASS IT ON**
Give a sponsee or newcomer their first three months free.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
No account. No sign-up. Your journal, inventories, and sober date stay on your device, with optional iCloud (iOS) or Google Drive (Android) backup.

Sober Dailies is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous World Services. It is a companion to the program — not a replacement for meetings, a sponsor, or medical care.

One day at a time.
